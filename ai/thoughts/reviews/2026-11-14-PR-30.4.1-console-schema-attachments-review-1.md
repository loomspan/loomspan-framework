### [P2] Stop stale attachment ranges before scope verification

- **Location:** `loomspan-console/web/src/observability/TraceRecords.tsx`, `AttachmentDetail` semantic range load.
- **Evidence:** The original effect only checked its `active` flag when publishing the final result/error. `readCompleteContent` invoked `verifyScope` and fetched subsequent ranges first. The real `TraceExplorer` verifier can mutate scope-mismatch state, reset selection and refresh target state.
- **Trigger:** Close attachment inspection, remove the record or switch trace/source/generation while a content range is pending, then resolve the old request. The reproduction closed inspection and resolved a partial response: scope verification ran twice and another range was requested after cleanup.
- **Impact:** Stale work can affect a newly selected evidence view through scope verification, despite correctly suppressing the final descriptor state. It also unnecessarily continues reading the closed detail.
- **Recommendation:** Check the mounted inspection lifetime before invoking the verifier and again after its asynchronous completion, so obsolete work exits before further ranges.
