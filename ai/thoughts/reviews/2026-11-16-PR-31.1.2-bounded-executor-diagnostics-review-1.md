### [P2] Derive message state from the content actually retained

- **Location:** `src/main/java/ai/loomspan/internal/core/BoundedExecutorDiagnosticCapture.java:86`
- **Evidence and trigger:** A message containing 3,000 control characters and data
  containing 32,000 ordinary characters each fit their individual budgets, but
  their escaped combined envelope exceeds 48 KiB. The fallback discarded data,
  retained the entire message, and unconditionally set messageState to TRUNCATED.
- **Impact:** Consumers would report missing message content even though all
  message content was retained, violating the explicit capture-state contract.
- **Correction:** Compare retained prefix length with original message length in
  the envelope fallback, just as the initial message capture does.

### [P2] Bound numeric rejection work before inspecting huge negative magnitudes

- **Location:** `src/main/java/ai/loomspan/internal/core/BoundedExecutorDiagnosticCapture.java:152`
- **Evidence and trigger:** The former bitLength guard could scan a negative
  power-of-two magnitude while determining its signed bit length. For example,
  an exact BigInteger holding negative 2^1,000,000 is already far outside the
  accepted range but its rejection work could scale with the supplied magnitude.
  Exact BigDecimal unscaled integers used the same guard.
- **Impact:** Numeric validation work was not limited by the accepted 4,096-bit
  representation budget before rejection.
- **Correction:** Compare exact integers against fixed minimum/maximum values.
  BigInteger magnitude-length comparison rejects huge values before digit scans;
  equal-length comparisons have a fixed maximum size. Preserve the signed
  bitLength range, including accepted negative 2^4096.
