## Code Review Findings

### [P2] Align the design checklist with the identity rules enforced by the public boundary

The capability-surface checklist at
`agent-skills/loomspan-docs/references/skill-authoring/checklists/evaluate-a-skill-design.md:38`
still required `^[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]{0,63}$` directly above the newly updated
root/catalog boundary guidance. Following that rule would permit `lookup_name`
or `Lookup`, which all public root and catalog operations reject, and would
exclude valid `find-order`. This contradicted the exact component rules now
documented and enforced for `SkillTemplate`, `SkillDescriptor`, `ValidatedSkill`
and namespace-scoped catalog lookup. The checklist is a planned and modified
guidance surface for this ticket.

This finding was resolved in this context. No actionable finding remains after
the final review of the resulting change.
