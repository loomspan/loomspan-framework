## Code Review Findings

### [P2] Preserve failed-envelope evidence when token counts cannot be represented

- **Location:** `src/main/java/ai/loomspan/internal/springai/SpringAiProviderIntegration.java`, failed-envelope usage conversion (now `failedResponseUsage` and `responseTokenCount`).
- **Evidence:** The initial candidate used `JsonNode.asInt(0)` for native counts before building the evidence-bearing `ProviderCallException`. A captured count of 4,294,967,296 narrowed to zero and produced an EXACT-zero usage record. Negative/inconsistent counts and component-sum overflow could instead throw before the original response/error evidence carrier was created.
- **Trigger:** A failed Responses HTTP-200 envelope supplies oversized, fractional, negative or inconsistent token counts, or an overflowing inferred total.
- **Impact:** Accounting could silently undercount usage; extraction failure could discard the original failed response and change its existing error-code retry classification.
- **Recommendation:** Convert counts without coercion/narrowing, retain the complete original envelope regardless of usage representability, and preserve existing provider failure classification. Unrepresentable usage must remain unavailable, without invented token counts.
