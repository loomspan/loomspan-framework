### 5. Exact schema and replay dependency limits

`ResponsesRequestBuilder.java:80` uses `ObjectMappers.jsonMapper()`. Output-schema decoding at `:228-229` reads SDK Schema directly; function-schema decoding at `:262` reads a raw Map. Both therefore use the pinned mapper's ordinary floating-point decoding before SDK serialization. No supported per-instance schema-mapper injection appears in the inspected builder/model/options path. The retained baseline/candidate README and source establish the narrow candidate mechanism: a **private rebuilt SDK-compatible Jackson 2 mapper with USE_BIG_DECIMAL_FOR_FLOATS** at both branches. The experiment does not mutate the singleton or alter integer decoding.

Retained reproduction: `docs/maintainers/probes/responses-schema-precision/README.md`, `upstream/ResponsesRequestBuilder.java`, baseline `SchemaWirePrecisionTest.java` and `SchemaWireFixTest.java`, candidate `SchemaWireFixTest.java`. Retained cases place nested bounds/enums in arrays, use long fractions/exponents, change schema between calls and retain large-integer/exact-decimal controls, strictness, effort/caps and stateless replay. Baseline passing tests assert the defect, not fidelity. These were not rerun in research.

Pinned `ResponsesItemMapper.reasoningItem()` (`:403-429`) skips missing/nonmatching opaque payload or missing item ID, reconstructs encrypted content and summary, and optionally text/status. `reasoningPart()` (`:623-634`) reads text, joined summary, ID/status and encrypted content; it does not retain OpenRouter's signature field. Summary-only/plaintext reasoning has no opaque payload and is skipped on replay. Multiple summary fragments join into one string (`:668-669`). Unknown item replay catches malformed JSON and omits it (`:466-473`). These actual mechanisms do not prove fidelity for every required signed/plaintext shape.

The accepted ticket explicitly says: if no acceptable artifact satisfies the owned contract, record the concrete dependency blocker and continue independent work without declaring completion. Copied upstream overrides, singleton mutation, another adapter, HTTP-body rewriting, weakened constraints and stringified numbers are excluded. No upstream submission was made or authorized. Planning must retain exact numeric and missing-required-reasoning acceptance as blocked/pending rather than representing existing encrypted-only success as complete acceptance.
