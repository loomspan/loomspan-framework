## Impacted areas and risks

Trace initialization occurs before binding installation; missing early metadata cannot be repaired later. Target frame/context ownership may differ from current thread binding during opening, closure, failure or cleanup. Retained execution must report old generations after replacement/removal/recreation. Mutable map aliases can corrupt installed metadata. Entry ID cannot silently become target ID. Equal local names and case-sensitive local names must remain distinct across metrics, correlations, lookups and displays. Unknown diagnostics and operation routes must not be classified as known skills.

Live/finalized/acquired/imported metadata, semantic readers, frontend contracts and shared corpus must agree. Required fields must not be fabricated by a legacy fallback. Observation auth must stay independent of call visibility. Payloads stay unchanged; only existing journal projection redaction is excepted. Earlier tests encode many obsolete unqualified references, so full-suite failure closure is required rather than a small green selection.

## Protected and intentionally changed paths

- **Application API:** closed allowlist and supported invocation/handoff/catalog/observer contracts; no new public dependency-map type or bean-replacement promise.
- **Supported SPI:** RestSkillHandler only; canonical target and captured target generation remain correct.
- **Configuration and manifest contracts:** complete publication, qualified reference grammar, Private default, strict publishable subset and independent runtimes already selected; update obsolete fixtures in place.
- **Persisted or serialized contracts:** same-release HTTP/SSE/portable NDJSON shapes intentionally update together, with exact-release saved-trace/live rejection retained. No old/new dual support or migrations.
- **Ephemeral diagnostic formats:** one stream/sequence, content fidelity, captured execution facts, null/unavailable behavior, retention and current reader coherence.
- **Internal implementation:** constructors, factories, projection records and Go intermediate models may change atomically without shims.
