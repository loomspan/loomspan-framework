Output decoding `:228-229` and tool Map decoding `:262` round fractional values before SDK serialization. New real Framework wire tests compare BigDecimal numeric values for both `text.format.schema` and `tools[].parameters`, including minimum/maximum/enum in nested arrays. Four parameter cases fail; large integer and exact 0.5 controls pass. For example `0.12345678901234567890123456789` becomes `0.12345678901234568`; `1.0000000000000001` becomes `1.0`. Three enabled real continuation fixtures assert plaintext/signed/summary-only required items survive and fail because no reasoning item is replayed. Ciphertext-only success cannot clear these requirements.

The retained numeric probe remains versioned at `docs/maintainers/probes/responses-schema-precision/README.md`, with exact upstream builder/source hashes and both decode sites. Its private SDK-compatible Jackson 2 mapper candidate uses `USE_BIG_DECIMAL_FOR_FLOATS` without integer-decoding changes. That isolated candidate is not shipped. No copied adapter class, singleton mutation, schema/body rewrite, numeric strings or weakened assertions were added. All new correctness failures stay enabled.
