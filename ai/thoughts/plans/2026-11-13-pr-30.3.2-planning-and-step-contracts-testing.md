## Risk Assessment

Primary risks are confusing plan and final-output schemas; accidentally broadening initial statuses; resetting correction budgets across parse/schema/semantic failures; swallowing operational/provider errors; changing omitted/nested-null arguments; allowing numeric rounding before assertions; leaking contract/options/continuation state across child/model overrides; and preserving obsolete schema-node/parser paths behind shims.

Application API stays unchanged and its allowlist remains authoritative. There is no supported SPI. Authored schemas, configuration/caps, RBAC, evidence/bindings and assignment semantics are protected. The generated internal plan JSON deliberately breaks YAML/fences/aliases, unknown/model-ID fields and omission of the new explicit required properties. Current-run traces must remain truthful and preserve original diagnostic content; no historical reader promise or content masking is introduced. Console protocol fixtures need no format change.
