## Change summary and impacted areas

- Replace remaining TypeSafe legacy descriptive-schema consumers with canonical standard schemas. Preserve native object-answer acceptance and request/retry/accounting behavior.
- Compile x-loomspan-evidence with existing expression rules and exact claim property names.
- Use faithful detached structural metadata for direct/forwarded catalogs and text display, independent of provider adaptations.
- Migrate stale test/accessor/vocabulary/no-coercion assumptions atomically and update authoring guidance using actual coverage.

Primary production owners are TypeSafeDeclaration, YamlSkillCatalog, EvidenceContract, CanonicalSchema and SkillDependencyValidator. Connected preserved owners include TypeSafeExecutor/Client, shared schema/input validation, evidence coverage/state, planning/repair/binding, catalogs, DTO mapper and Console text UI.

## Risk assessment and contract treatment

The highest risks are accidental TypeSafe response validation or extra attempts, lost precision in any serialization/trace step, name normalization leaking into exact claim lookup, incorrect null/absence treatment, inheritance of child-local evidence through forwarding, and fixed-field/provider-specific keyword loss. Broad fixture restoration must preserve semantic assertions rather than weaken them to get a green build.

Application API signatures and native protocol behavior are protected by the ticket. Manifest annotation vocabulary and exact/no-coercion semantics are explicitly migrated. Internal factory/accessor types are intentionally removed without shims; there is no Supported SPI. Current-run diagnostics retain content, paths/columns, usage/failure facts and original source. Existing journal projection redaction is preserved only at its established boundary. Existing REST/Go/TypeScript DTO fields and version marker remain unchanged; changed schema-string fixtures must be coordinated with their existing consumers.
